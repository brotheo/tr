The exam rule

Follow this order every time — never pick a loop because you remember its syntax.

1
Read the scenario — all of it, before deciding anything.
2
Identify the clue word — the phrase that tells you how the repetition is controlled.
3
Choose the loop — based on the clue, not on habit.
4
Only then write the pseudocode — the structure comes last, not first.
