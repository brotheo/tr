Teaching a student who is still learning English

  • Code first, words second. Let him show understanding by running and changing code. Accept short or one-word answers in class.
  • Use the key-word table at the top of his page. Point at it each time you say key, value or pair.
  • Short sentences, one idea each. Avoid idioms such as "look it up" or "under the hood". Say "use the key to get the value".
  • Check with predictions, not "Do you understand?" Learners often say yes when they don't understand. The predict boxes and gap fills show you what he actually knows.
  • Written exam answers: his page has sentence starters (yellow boxes) for questions like "Explain…". Practise saying the full sentence out loud once.
  • The 中文 hints are a support, not the main text. Once he is confident, encourage him to turn them off for the challenges.
  • The examples use neutral, everyday topics (pets, milk tea, fruit, phone books, school marks) and a mix of names: Wei, Anna, Tom, Ploy.

What is a dictionary?

PART 2

Add, change, delete

Predict: final menu {'coffee': 50} · p 40

True or false: 1 F · 2 T · 3 T · 4 T · 5 F (get returns None)

Key idead[key] = value adds the key if it is new and changes the value if the key is already there. Ask him to say this in his own words, in English or Chinese.
PART 3 & 4

Looping, and dict or list

Gaps: scores[name] · scores.items() · count + 1

Choose: temperatures List · English → Chinese Dict · student number → name Dict · queue List · fruit → amount Dict
